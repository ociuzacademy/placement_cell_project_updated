Connecting Talent with Opportunity

CampusHire is a unified placement platform that brings students, tutors, and administrators together in one place. From the first job application to the final interview, we provide the tools, structure, and support needed to turn academic potential into real-world careers.

Built for colleges and universities, CampusHire streamlines the entire placement journey — from publishing job postings and organizing orientation sessions to tracking applications and guiding students through every stage of their career development.

We believe that every student deserves access to the right opportunities, every tutor deserves the tools to support them, and every institution deserves a platform that makes placement seamless.

Who We Serve

Three connected roles, one seamless platform

For Students

Launch your career with curated job listings, one-click applications, and complete visibility into every opportunity you pursue.

  • Browse and search active job openings
  • Apply directly and track status in real time
  • Register for orientation and training sessions
  • Maintain a complete academic profile
For Tutors

Guide your students through every stage of the placement journey with tools to monitor, review, and support them at scale.

  • View all students assigned to you
  • Review job applications and approve or reject
  • Track session registrations and progress
  • Export student lists for offline review
For Administrators

Run the entire platform from one dashboard — from job postings and tutor approvals to departments and platform-wide reporting.

  • Create and manage job postings
  • Approve or reject tutor registrations
  • Schedule orientation and training sessions
  • Manage departments, courses, and exports
