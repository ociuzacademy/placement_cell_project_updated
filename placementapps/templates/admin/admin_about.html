Admin Panel

Managing CampusHire, One Connection at a Time

The CampusHire Admin Panel is the operational heart of the platform. From here, administrators manage job postings, approve tutor registrations, organize orientation sessions, and oversee the departments and programs that power the entire placement ecosystem.

Our goal is to keep the platform smooth, secure, and useful for both students and companies. Every approval, every department, and every session published from this panel brings a student one step closer to their career.

Built on the principles of transparency and efficiency, the Admin Panel gives you complete visibility into platform activity — from the number of pending tutor applications to the list of students registered for each orientation session.
